Label every visible fish

Per fish click head (snout tip), "centre" (just behind the head, at the pectoral fins, about 1/5 of the way from snout to tail: where SLEAP puts it; not the middle of the body), tail (tip). A partly hidden fish: label only the visible part (skip a hidden point with s). A fish you cannot see at all: no label. Points shown when the frame opens are SLEAP's; correct, delete or keep them.
