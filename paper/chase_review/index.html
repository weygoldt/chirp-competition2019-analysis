    what to mark, keys

    Mark where you see the chase start and end, frame by frame. Your marks start on the BORIS marks; move them (keys or drag), or save them unchanged if you agree. Chirps are shown for orientation and cannot be edited. To mark without the annotator's times, untick "show BORIS markers" before opening a chase (the window is then widened by 0-8 s at each end, at random, so its edges do not give the times away). Blind, you mark every chase in the window (o … f for each), not "the" chase: the export matches your chases to the BORIS chases by overlap. A chase already running when the window starts: its onset on the first frame (Home, o); still running at the end: e. To mark from the video alone, also untick "show chirps". Each save records whether markers and chirps were visible. Nothing in the dataset is changed: saves go to revisions.jsonl.
